# Product safety statement

SwasthyaPath provides public-health navigation and operational information. It does not diagnose, prescribe, predict disease, replace clinical judgment, guarantee service availability, or confirm that a facility has accepted a referral.

Emergency actions remain available without authentication. Clinical screening may run only from a published, non-expired protocol reviewed by a named licensed clinician. When a red-flag rule matches, normal ranking stops and the interface presents the approved emergency action. When data or rules are missing, stale, malformed, or unavailable, the product fails closed and does not substitute demo or community-submitted information.

Every public facility field must be traceable to evidence and a verification date. Users may choose an alternative facility. Ranking is deterministic: required verified capability, operating-hours compatibility when verified, public/free preference, travel estimate, and freshness. “Unknown” is distinct from “available” and “unavailable.”

Launch requires documented clinician approval, bilingual human review, verified emergency-number wording, facility verification coverage, security/privacy review, backup restoration, and incident rehearsal.
